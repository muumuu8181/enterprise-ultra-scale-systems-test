# Movie Distribution Management System

## Overview
A comprehensive system for movie distribution companies to manage box office revenue, distribution, promotion, and digital delivery.

## Modules

### Distribution Management
- **Work Management**: Distribution contracts, screening rights, distribution regions
- **Theater Contracts**: Screening contracts, revenue sharing conditions, minimum guarantees
- **Screening Schedule**: Release date, late shows, screening period

### Box Office Revenue Management
- **Daily Aggregation**: Box office revenue by theater, attendance
- **Revenue Calculation**: Distribution revenue (deducting theater share from box office revenue)
- **Ranking**: Weekend box office ranking

### Promotion
- **Promotion Materials**: Trailers, posters, flyers
- **Promotion Costs**: TV CM, newspaper ads, Web ads
- **Preview Screenings**: Press previews, general previews

### Digital Distribution
- **DCP (Digital Cinema Package)**: Distribution
- **KDM (Key Delivery Message)**: Encryption key distribution
- **Screening Logs**: Number of screenings, audience record

### Technical Requirements
- **Digital Cinema**: DCI standard compliance
- **Security**: Anti-piracy, watermarking
